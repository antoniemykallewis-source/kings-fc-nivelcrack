Sources & proposal notes

Campaign status

Sacramento Kings, Nivelcrack, and Street Soccer USA partnerships are agreed, per the project brief. Talent participation, commercial placements, campaign deliverables, usage rights, distribution commitments, venue, and launch date remain to be finalized.

Supplied materials

  • SKFC25_RangePlan_Final_260210.pdf, page 2: purple HOME jersey. The reference collection is labelled 2025; this proposal makes no launch-date claim.
  • About NivelCrack.pdf, pages 2–11: brand positioning, projects, clients, community activations, and media connections.
  • Instagram screenshots supplied October 8, 2026: Djibril Cissé 2.8M and Marco Borriello 956K followers. The approximate 3.76M sum is neither unique audience nor projected reach.

The Kings collaboration

The Sacramento Kings are Sacramento’s NBA team, playing at Golden 1 Center. The collaboration is confirmed in the project brief. “First-of-its-kind” describes the campaign ambition, not a verified historical first across sport.

The community story

  • Street Soccer USA: nonprofit mission. A national 501(c)(3) nonprofit using soccer for community development.
  • Sacramento State: Lisa Wrightsman and Tiffany Fraser’s story, October 29, 2021. Photographs by Hrach Avetisyan / Sacramento State.
  • Street Soccer USA: Sacramento programs. Launch-section image is a Sacramento program reference, not a photo of the proposed arena event.

Creative and commercial scope

The hero jersey is an AI-assisted dimensional interpretation of the supplied design; the interactive jersey study uses the original artwork. Both sponsor placements are concepts. The proposed package funds campaign production and content; no donation amount, direct program funding, charitable outcome, endorsement, or guaranteed editorial coverage is promised.

References to prior Nivelcrack collaborators describe its portfolio only. Talent-related filming, appearances, posts, likeness use, and paid amplification require separate confirmation. Final campaign rights and quantities will be documented in the partnership agreement.

Proposal prepared October 8, 2026. Research and art-direction references are included in the project documentation.
