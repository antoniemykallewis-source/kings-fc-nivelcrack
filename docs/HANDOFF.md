Prepared October 8, 2026.

## Presentation

Lead with the jersey and the line “The world is our home court.” Then connect the community story to global football culture, show the proposed launch moment, and close on the sponsor's usable campaign assets.

The sponsor is buying a role in the story and a defined creative package. The strongest conversation is about what their brand wants people to feel and do, then the rights and distribution needed to make that happen.

Use the chapter numbers on desktop to present as a deck. The mobile version scrolls naturally. “Explore the kit” opens the original purple artwork; entering a sponsor name updates the proposed front placement. The front/back buttons preserve the original kit reference. The world controls rotate the projected globe. The sponsor tabs separate content, brand placement, and measurement.
